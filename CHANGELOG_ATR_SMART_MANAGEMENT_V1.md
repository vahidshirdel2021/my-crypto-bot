# ATR Smart Management V1

- ATR is used as a volatility ruler inside Smart Management, not as the primary SL/TP engine.
- Structural Swing remains the SL anchor; structure remains the TP/entry framework.

- Strong/extreme adverse ATR pressure can trigger an early risk-reduction exit when the trade is already losing and weakness evidence is present.
- Existing MFE profit protection and weakness management remain active.
- 5m/15m Structure First logic is unchanged.
